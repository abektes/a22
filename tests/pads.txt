# Chord pads in fm1-base's host simulator: the device app, the platform simulated.
#   ./build.sh --host, then app/build/host/a22_host tests/pads.txt app/build/host
# ARP held opens CHORDS (said with its columns), K1 turns MODE to PADS, K2 the set; the first white key sends its
# chord's three notes on USB (POP 1 pad 1 in C: 48 52 55, A22's panel keys at 127) and its off. Never presses SAVE.
wait 600
listen
press ARP
wait 800
release ARP
wait 400
said PAGE. MODE
spin K1 1
wait 600
said MODE PADS
spin K2 1
wait 600
said SET POP 2
spin K2 -1
wait 600
out
key 0 down
wait 300
expect_out USB 90 30 7F
expect_out USB 90 34 7F
expect_out USB 90 37 7F
shot pads.png
key 0 up
wait 300
expect_out USB 80 30 00
spin SELECT 1
wait 600
said PLAY
shot chordplay.png
