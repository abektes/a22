# Settings and a playing tape (run by tests/run_tests.sh in fm1-base's host simulator: ./build.sh --host, then
# app/build/host/a22_host tests/statesave.txt app/build/host). A state save erases a flash sector, which stalls the sound,
# so on its own it waits until nothing sounds (app.c: h1_quiet). SAVE says when: it saves at once, playing or not.
wait 600
listen
wait 100
# a take on T1; the tape plays on
tap REC
wait 100
key 12 down
wait 150
key 12 up
wait 300
tap REC
wait 1500
# 1  an engine changed while the tape plays, no SAVE: not written (no stall mid-play), so a power cut loses it
turn PRESET 1
wait 200
said ELECTRIC. RHODES
wait 5000
power
wait 800
listen
wait 100
turn ALGO 1
wait 300
said FM-4. BASS
# (the tape is stopped after the start, so that change is saved in silence)
wait 3000
# 2  the tape playing again, an engine changed and SAVE tapped: written at once, and kept through a power cut
tap PLAY
wait 1000
turn PRESET 1
wait 200
said ELECTRIC. RHODES
tap SAVE
wait 1000
power
wait 800
listen
wait 100
turn ALGO 1
wait 300
said ELECTRIC. WURLI
# 3  and the tape itself came through: PLAY plays it
tap PLAY
peakreset
wait 3000
expect peak_db_min -50
