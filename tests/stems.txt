# Stems in fm1-base's host simulator: the USB audio input's ten channels, the mix on 1-2 and T1 to T4 on 3-4 to 9-10.
#   ./build.sh --host, then app/build/host/a22_host tests/stems.txt app/build/host, then
#   tools/a22_stems.py app/build/host/stems.wav --check
# A take on each track, a key each, about 1.2 s apart (the tape is 4 bars at 118: 8.1 s), then a pass recorded with
# MASTER at half and FX bypassed, a loud chord played by hand on T4 over its end (the limiter works); then T1 muted and
# a pass recorded with FX on. tools/a22_stems.py checks each track on its own pair, in order, and the stems adding up
# to the mix. Never presses SAVE.

wait 60
release EDIT

tap REC

key 16 down

key 16 up

tap REC

press EDIT
wait 60

wait 60
release EDIT
wait 300
tap REC

wait 400

press EDIT
wait 60
