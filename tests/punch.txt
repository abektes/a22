# PUNCH in fm1-base's host simulator: the device app, the platform simulated.
#   ./build.sh --host, then app/build/host/a22_host tests/punch.txt app/build/host
# Hold FX and a snapshot key (the eight white keys from the lowest F: keys 0 2 4 6 7 9 11 12) and the reader says
# PUNCH, then the punch's name; the key plays no note, any other key does; FX let go after a punch opens no page.
# (key 4 is note 57 = 39, key 1 is 54 = 36; A22 plays panel keys at 127 = 7F.) Never presses SAVE.
wait 600
listen
# FX tapped alone: no PUNCH said (a tap is no hold), and its page opens as it is let go
tap FX
wait 300
unsaid PUNCH
tap HOME
wait 300
tap FX
wait 300
said PAGE
tap HOME
wait 300
said HOME
# FX held: the strip is up, and once it is a hold the reader says so
out
press FX
wait 600
said PUNCH
# a snapshot key is a punch: the third, ST 1/16. It plays no note
key 4 down
wait 300
said ST 1/16
expect_no_out USB 90 39 7F
# a key that is no punch plays its note, the punch holds on
key 1 down
wait 300
key 1 up
wait 100
expect_out USB 90 36 7F
# a second punch key replaces the first: the seventh, CRUSH
key 11 down
wait 300
said CRUSH
key 11 up
wait 100
# FX let go with a punch held: no page opens (and the first key still holds, so its punch goes on)
release FX
wait 300
unsaid PAGE
key 4 up
wait 300
unsaid PAGE
where
wait 100
said HOME
# FX tapped alone again: the page opens, as before
tap FX
wait 300
said PAGE
